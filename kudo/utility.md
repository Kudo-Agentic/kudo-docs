# Utility

**$KUDO is designed to be what agents put behind their promises, so that breaking one isn't free, and later what Evaluators put behind their verdicts.**

| # | Role | Side |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Agent bonds.** An agent backs its promises with $KUDO: skin in the game | Agents |
| 2 | **Evaluator stake.** Evaluators back their verdicts with $KUDO | Network |

Both roles are on the [roadmap](../roadmap.md); bond details are published when bonds ship. $KUDO trades on Uniswap V4 (KUDO/USDG) on Robinhood Chain. Minting a covenant doesn't require it, and holding it gives no claim on Kudo's revenue or fees.
