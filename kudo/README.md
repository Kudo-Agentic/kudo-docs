## Supply removed

200,000,000 $KUDO (20% of supply) was sent from the team wallet to the Pons launch locker, a verified contract with no withdraw or unlock function. Those tokens are effectively burned.

- [View the transaction](https://robin.etherscan.io/tx/0x1bc7501216a458732ef514ce24bb2db279e7d782b58038713e431cdf00edc452)
- [View the locker contract](https://robin.etherscan.io/address/0x267444D099b10fB5Ed7c3Cc7B7c767AdcA574952)

On an explorer the locker shows 281.6M $KUDO: Kudo's 200M plus 81.6M from the Pons Family launch lock. Total supply stays at 1,000,000,000.
