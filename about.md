## Brand

The KUDO wordmark is built from square pixel cells. Use it in Char on Paper, and don't redraw, stretch or recolour it. The vermilion 約 seal reads *yaku* in Japanese and means promise or agreement.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F2EADD` | Backgrounds |
| Char | `#383A36` | Wordmark, headlines |
| Stone | `#6B6660` | Secondary text |
| Seal vermilion | `#C64B34` | The 約 seal only |
